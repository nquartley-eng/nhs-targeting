Two ways to rank

Propensity is the model's chance that a practice becomes a top-20% prescriber per patient on the register. It finds receptive practices, which suits early adopters, advocates and speaker programmes.

Opportunity multiplies that chance by the register size, so it points to where the prescriptions will be. Use it for sales coverage, and for niche or later-line drugs, where most practices never prescribe and size decides who does.

How well it worked on past launches

Each training launch (diabetes, AF, heart failure) was hidden in turn, the model was trained on the other two, and the hidden launch was scored. COPD triple inhalers (2017) and vortioxetine (2015) were then scored by the finished model without any retraining. What it can't see

The strongest driver of uptake is local: hospital specialists and area formularies. On launch day that shows up only indirectly, through how quickly neighbouring practices took up earlier new drugs. Expect a useful edge over random visits, not certainty. Refreshing the scores after the first three months of real prescribing would sharpen them.
